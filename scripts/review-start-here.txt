WHAT THIS IS
The redesigned homepage for ghlmegaminds.com. This is phase 1: once the
homepage direction is approved, the same design is rolled out to the
other pages.

VIEW IT ONLINE
https://ghl-megaminds-redesign.vercel.app
(the same page as below, works on phone and desktop)

HOW TO VIEW IT FROM THIS FOLDER
1. Unzip this folder.
2. Open the "site" folder and double-click index.html. It opens in your
   browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge and Firefox all work).
3. Scroll slowly from top to bottom: sections, numbers and illustrations
   animate as they come into view. Hover over the buttons and cards too.
4. Make the browser window narrow to see the mobile layout.

Prefer pictures? The "screenshots" folder has the full page on desktop
and mobile, plus frames showing the animations.

PLEASE NOTE
- Only the homepage is built so far. Menu and footer links to other pages
  (About, Contact, Blog and so on) do not open in this copy.
- The booking calendar at the bottom is your live HighLevel calendar and
  needs an internet connection. Any booking made there is a real
  appointment.
- Analytics and the chat widget are switched off in this review copy.
- The video testimonials are placeholders: stock sample clips marked
  "Sample" and "Client name". They are not real clients and will not go
  on the live site. Send us your real client videos (with each client's
  name, role and permission) and they will replace them.
